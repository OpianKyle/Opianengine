1. General Definitions
Policyholder: The individual whose name appears on the policy or cover document.

Beneficiary: The person(s) entitled to claim benefits under the cover.

Cover: Refers to the insured benefits listed in this agreement.

Waiting Period: A period after the policy start date during which certain benefits may not be claimable.

Accidental Event: An unforeseen, external, violent, and visible event leading to injury or death.

2. Covered Products and Benefits
2.1 Funeral Cover
Provides a lump sum upon the death of the policyholder or registered dependents to assist with funeral-related costs.

Waiting Period: Applies for natural death; none for accidental death.

Exclusions: Death due to suicide within a specific period, criminal acts, or undeclared pre-existing conditions.

2.2 Accidental Death Cover
Offers a benefit in the event of death caused by an accidental event.

Immediate coverage unless otherwise specified in the policy schedule.

Exclusions: Death from intentional self-harm, substance abuse, or illegal activities.

2.3 Funeral Assist
Support services such as:

Funeral planning assistance

Discounted funeral-related services

Grief counselling

2.4 Family Income Benefit
Monthly income provided to a nominated beneficiary for a specified number of months after the policyholder’s death.

Designed to assist the family with ongoing living expenses.

2.5 EMS Assist (Emergency Medical Services)
Access to 24/7 ambulance and emergency medical response for accidents or life-threatening events.

Usage limits may apply per member per annum.

2.6 Legal Assist / Lawyer Assist
Telephonic legal consultation and assistance with:

Civil, labour, and criminal legal matters

Contracts and documentation

Legal letters and advice

Limits may apply to the number of consultations per year.

2.7 Repatriation Cover
Transport of mortal remains to the town or province of burial.

Available to the policyholder and registered dependents.

2.8 Celebrate Life
May include wellness benefits, vouchers, discounts, or rewards.

Subject to availability and change without prior notice.

2.9 24/7 Nurse On-Call
Access to qualified nurses for telephonic health advice, symptom checks, and medical triage support.

2.10 Virtual GP Assistant
Access to online general practitioners via phone or video consultation.

Not suitable for emergency treatment.

Usage limits may apply.

2.11 Medical Second Opinion
Access to expert medical professionals for a second opinion on a diagnosis or treatment.

Requires submission of relevant medical documents.

2.12 Crime Victim Assist
Support following criminal incidents such as hijacking, theft, or assault.

Includes trauma counselling and procedural guidance for reporting to authorities.

2.13 Assault & Trauma Assist
Trauma counselling and short-term support following incidents of assault or severe emotional distress.

Session limits may apply.

2.14 Emergency Medical Services
Nationwide access to emergency medical treatment and ambulance services for qualifying emergencies.

3. Eligibility
Available to individuals who meet the age and residency requirements at the time of policy activation.

Dependents must be declared and registered under the policy.

4. Claims Process
Claims must be submitted within a reasonable period after the event.

Required documents include:

Valid identification

Completed claim form

Supporting documentation (e.g., death certificate, police report)

Processing time may take up to 14 working days after receipt of all valid documents.

5. Exclusions (Applicable to All Benefits)
Fraudulent claims or misrepresentation

Death or injury related to illegal activities

Pre-existing conditions not declared at the time of application

Self-inflicted harm or suicide (within applicable waiting period)

War, riots, civil unrest, or acts of terrorism

6. Cancellation and Lapse
Non-payment of premiums for a specified consecutive period may lead to policy lapse.

The policyholder may cancel the policy by providing written notice with at least 30 days’ notice.
